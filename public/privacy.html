1. Who we are

[Company] ("we", "us") operates DynaQR and is the data controller for personal data processed through the service. Contact: [contact email].

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and an access token we generate.
  • Link data: the titles and destination URLs you create.
  • Scan data: see section 3.
  • Billing data: if you subscribe, our payment processor (Stripe) collects your payment details. We do not store full card numbers; we store a customer reference and your plan status.

3. Scan data & tracking

When someone scans one of your QR codes (i.e. requests /r/<code>), the service records, for analytics purposes:

  • a timestamp,
  • the referring URL (if the browser sends one),
  • the browser/device user-agent string.

We do not use third-party advertising trackers, and we do not store the full IP address of scanners in this build. If you (the account owner) enable additional tracking or pass scan data to third parties, you become responsible for disclosing that to the people scanning your codes. Where required, you must provide your own notice to scan end-users that scanning is logged.

4. How we use data

  • To operate the redirect and QR features you request;
  • To produce scan analytics for the account that owns a link;
  • To manage subscriptions and prevent abuse;
  • To communicate service and billing information.

We do not sell personal data.

5. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contract: to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the service, prevent abuse, and produce aggregate analytics for account owners.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable law.

7. Retention

We keep account, link, and scan data until you delete the link or your account, after which it is removed from the active database. Billing records may be retained as required by tax and accounting law.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. You can delete individual links in the app, or delete your entire account and associated data via DELETE /api/account (the "Delete account" action in the dashboard). To exercise other rights, contact [contact email]. EU/UK users may also lodge a complaint with their supervisory authority.

9. Cookies & local storage

The app stores your access token in your browser's localStorage so you stay signed in. This is strictly necessary for the service to function and is not used for advertising. The redirect endpoint does not set tracking cookies on scanners.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data, including HTTPS in production and scoped access tokens. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 (or the relevant age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect their data.
